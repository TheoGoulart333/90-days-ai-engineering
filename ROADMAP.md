# Roadmap

This roadmap defines the direction of the journey. It is intentionally adaptable: the work may change as new questions, project needs, and opportunities produce stronger evidence.

| Phase | Primary question | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01 · Foundation** | What fundamentals and habits make AI Engineering work reliable? | Current |
| **02 · Application** | How do those fundamentals become useful systems? | Upcoming |
| **03 · Proof and contribution** | What evidence demonstrates durable growth? | Upcoming |

## 01 · Foundation

**Focus:** core concepts, engineering discipline, and reliable documentation.

- AI Engineering fundamentals and modern workflows
- Python, APIs, data handling, and production-minded software practices
- Prompting, structured outputs, tool use, and retrieval patterns
- Evaluation, testing, observability, and responsible implementation
- Clear daily records and small, reproducible experiments

## 02 · Application

**Focus:** building useful systems instead of isolated demos.

- AI-powered product experiments
- End-to-end projects with readable architecture
- Trade-offs, failure modes, latency, cost, and user experience
- Technical notes that explain implementation decisions
- Public project walkthroughs and demonstrations

## 03 · Proof and contribution

**Focus:** turning the accumulated work into durable professional evidence.

- Complete and polish the strongest projects
- Pursue relevant certifications when they support the learning goals
- Make thoughtful Open Source contributions
- Consolidate badges, certificates, PRs, and public posts
- Publish a final review of progress, gaps, and next steps

## Completion criteria

The challenge is complete when 90 consecutive days have been documented honestly, with the available evidence linked from the repository. Certificates, badges, projects, and PRs are optional evidence—not quotas.
